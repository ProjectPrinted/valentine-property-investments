About Valentine Property Investments

We provide first class lawn care and property maintenance for residential and commercial properties in the Lehigh Valley, and we invest in and restore property too.

Our approach

We put in the time to give every property a full manicure. That means paying attention to the details, scheduling work around your needs, and keeping prices affordable without cutting corners.

Whether it's a family home or a commercial property, we treat it the way we'd want ours treated.

Grounded in Quality

Our tagline isn't a slogan, it's an operating standard. We're committed to having every job done to your complete satisfaction, and we don't consider it finished until you're happy with it.

Property investment and restoration

Alongside our maintenance work, Valentine Property Investments acquires and restores residential and commercial properties. We look for properties where careful restoration and attentive care can create lasting value, and we bring the same attention to detail to every one.
